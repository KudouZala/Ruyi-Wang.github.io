About

I am Ruyi Wang (王濡翼), a master's student at Tongji University, working at the intersection of Embodied AI, Robot Learning, Reinforcement Learning, and Physics-informed Machine Learning.

My recent work focuses on Humanoid and Legged Locomotion, including PPO-based Policy Training, Imitation Learning, Motion Retargeting, and Sim2Real / Sim2Sim Transfer on platforms such as Isaac Gym, MuJoCo, and ROS2.

I also conduct AI4Science research on Physics-structured World Models for PEM Water Electrolyzers, focusing on Multimodal Time-series Modeling and Electrochemical Diagnosis.

My work has been published in Journal of Power Sources (2026) and Discover Applied Sciences (2025).

I am actively seeking opportunities in Embodied AI, Robotics, and Reinforcement Learning.
